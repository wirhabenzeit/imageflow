We reached the coast in the long light of evening. Across the water, the ridge seemed to rise straight from the fjord. Cloud shadows moved across its face while the village below stayed bright.

On the path above Fjordgård, each turn opened a different view of the same water. We stopped where the birches gave way to bare stone and watched the light move across the far shore.

*By the time we turned back, the fjord had gone almost still.*
