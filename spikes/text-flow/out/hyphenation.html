The road narrowed as it followed the fjord. By the time we reached the last bend, the clouds had dropped low enough to hide the mountains, leaving just the water and a white house on the shore. We stopped the car, turned off the engine, and listened to nothing at all for a long while. Senja is often described as *Norway in miniature*, a phrase that sounds like a brochure until you drive across it in a single afternoon and pass through farmland, birch forest, bare granite, and a coastline that looks as if it had been torn rather than cut. The island sits off the coast of Troms, connected to the mainland by a bridge at Gisund, and most visitors arrive the way we did: late, tired, and slightly unprepared for how quickly the light changes.

Die Straße wurde schmaler, als sie dem Fjord folgte. Als wir die letzte Kurve erreichten, hingen die Wolken so tief, dass sie die Berge verdeckten und nur das Wasser und ein weißes Haus am Ufer übrig ließen. Fotografinnen verbringen ganze Berufsleben damit, dieses gleichmäßige Licht im Studio nachzubilden.
