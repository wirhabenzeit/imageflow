# Checking and exporting

## Book check

The Book check checks the whole book after every change: missing content, text that does not fit, photos below the template's minimum resolution, and problems the template reports itself. An icon at the right of the status bar shows its progress and the number of issues. Click it to list the findings, with links to the affected spreads. **Book → Check Book** opens the same list.

Errors prevent printing; warnings are worth reading before you order.

## Proof PDF

**File → Export PDF…** (⌘E) saves the book as it looks now, for review on screen or a home printer. Pages that use photos whose originals are unavailable need confirmation and carry a visible warning.

## Print PDF

**File → Export for Print…** (⇧⌘E) checks the whole book against the template's print format before it saves, and reduces photos to the format's maximum resolution. Its preflight reports the page count, photo resolution, missing content, text that is not placed and problems in the PDF.

## Print formats

The template defines the formats a book can be printed in, with trim size, bleed and safe area. Choose the format in the template settings in the left sidebar. The Photobook starter offers a 290 × 290 mm square and a 320 × 240 mm landscape format, both with 3 mm bleed on the outside, top and bottom; the Wall calendar comes in A3 and A2.

Before ordering a full book, export a print PDF, upload it to your print service and order a single proof if you can. Template authors can read about formats in [Print format](../template-api/print-formats.md).
