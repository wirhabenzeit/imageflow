# Checking and exporting

## Book check

The Book check runs after each change. It reports missing content, text that does not fit, low-resolution photos and template findings. Click its status-bar icon or choose **Book → Check Book** (⇧⌘K) to see issues and jump to affected spreads.

Review errors and warnings before ordering a printed book.

## Export PDF

When the selected format offers an exterior cover, choose **Cover** in the toolbar and select a layout in the Inspector. Use **Guides** to see the trim, safe areas, and folds. An unfinished content page count shows a provisional sheet. The Cover preview tells you the page count it uses.

Use **Export Cover PDF…** in Cover and **Export Content PDF…** in Content to save the two printer files. Cover export requires a supported interior page count and a selected cover layout.

Choose **File → Export PDF…** (⌘E), or click **Export PDF…** in the toolbar. Export uses the book's print format, including trim size and bleed, and reduces photos to its maximum resolution. It checks page count, photo resolution, missing content, unplaced text, PDF fonts, transparency restrictions and file-size limits.

If there are errors, export lists them and offers **Cancel** or **Export Anyway**. Exporting anyway saves the PDF with those problems; pages with unavailable originals are marked and may use cached previews or placeholders. Warnings remain in Book check and do not require confirmation.

A rendering failure that prevents creating a usable PDF still stops export.

## Print formats

Choose the format in the book settings in the left sidebar. The template defines trim size, bleed, safe area and output requirements.

Photobook offers WhiteWall Gallery Square, Blurb Large Square with a Premium Matte dust jacket, and a 320 × 240 mm landscape format. Wall calendar offers A3 and A2 landscape formats.

Upload a print PDF to your print service and order a proof before a full run. For template authors, see [Print format](../template-api/print-formats.md).
